Why Pacific Beach

Why Choose Pacific Beach?

Pacific Beach puts the school, the beach and many everyday services in one walkable neighborhood — and CEL offers one shared-apartment option within walking distance of class. That is the short answer, and it is why students choose our English school in San Diego.

What you get in Pacific Beach

Where classes are

Classes take place in the center of Pacific Beach, close to the places you’ll use after school.

Location & Walkability

Where Is CEL San Diego?

CEL San Diego is located at 945 Hornblend Street, one block from Garnet Avenue, in the heart of Pacific Beach — a beach neighborhood between Mission Bay and La Jolla, in San Diego, California, USA.

Getting Here from the Airport

You can reach Pacific Beach from San Diego International Airport (SAN) by rideshare, taxi, public transport or a pre-booked CEL airport transfer. By car it takes about 15 minutes, depending on traffic.

What Can You Reach on Foot?

Many of the places you’ll use every day are within walking distance:

Garnet Avenue

Pacific Beach’s main street, one block from the campus at 945 Hornblend Street: cafés, restaurants, supermarkets and nightlife.

The beach and boardwalk

The beach is about a five-minute walk; the Ocean Front Boardwalk runs about three miles (5 km) along the beachfront to Crystal Pier, starting at the foot of Garnet Avenue.

English & Surf

CEL’s English & Surf program uses a surf school within walking distance of the campus.

Walkable accommodation

The CEL Shared Apartment Superior Pacific Beach is a 25-minute walk from school.

The Campus Itself

Classes average 7 students, with a maximum of 12 — small enough to give you frequent opportunities to speak and individual feedback. The campus has 14 air-conditioned classrooms, a student lounge and free Wi-Fi. CEL is accredited by the CEA and was granted the maximum 10-year accreditation period — independent confirmation that the school meets recognized standards for English-language teaching. CEL has taught English in San Diego since 1981; since 2025, all San Diego courses run from the Pacific Beach campus.

Housing & Transport

Where Do CEL Students Live?

CEL accommodation starts at $270 per week. The Superior shared apartments in Pacific Beach are a 25-minute walk from the school; every other CEL residence is no more than a 20-minute bus ride away — only additional summer residences can be further. Homestays are in residential areas further out. All regular CEL shared apartments in San Diego are managed by CEL itself.

Do You Need a Car?

You don’t need a car to attend classes or handle most everyday needs in Pacific Beach. Public transport is there when you want to explore the wider city.

Is Pacific Beach Safe?

Pacific Beach is a busy residential beach neighborhood — students, families and young professionals live here year-round, and the streets around the school and along Garnet Avenue are active through the day and evening. Day to day, the atmosphere is relaxed. All CEL housing sits more than a block off the nightlife strip. For the bigger picture, read our guide: Is San Diego safe for international students?

Student Life

What Is Student Life Like in Pacific Beach?

Class first, beach after

The rhythm is simple: class first, beach after. Depending on the season and your schedule, classes run in the morning or afternoon — either way, the neighborhood takes over when they end: the beach, the boardwalk, the school’s activity program.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Where exactly is CEL San Diego?

+

CEL San Diego is at 945 Hornblend Street, one block from Garnet Avenue — the main street of Pacific Beach.

Is Pacific Beach a good area for international students?

+

Yes. The school, the beach, cafés, supermarkets and other everyday services are within the neighborhood, and CEL offers one apartment option within walking distance of class. One thing to know: Garnet Avenue is lively on weekend nights; all CEL housing sits more than a block off the strip.

Can I walk to class from my accommodation?

+

From the CEL Shared Apartment Superior Pacific Beach it is a 25-minute walk. Every other CEL residence is no more than a 20-minute bus ride away (only additional summer residences can be further); homestays are further out, depending on the host family’s location.

Do I need a car in Pacific Beach?

+

No. You can attend classes and handle most everyday needs without a car; public transport covers trips beyond the neighborhood.

How do I get from San Diego Airport (SAN) to Pacific Beach?

+

By rideshare, taxi, public transport or a pre-booked CEL airport transfer. By car the trip takes about 15 minutes, depending on traffic.

Is Pacific Beach safe?

+

Pacific Beach is a busy residential beach neighborhood with a relaxed day-to-day atmosphere — students, families and young professionals live here year-round. All CEL housing is more than a block from the nightlife strip. More detail: our guide “Is San Diego safe for international students?”
