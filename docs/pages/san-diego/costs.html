Quick Answer

What Does It Cost to Study English in San Diego?

Studying English at CEL San Diego costs US$300–$410 per week in tuition, depending on course intensity and duration. A typical 12-week stay including accommodation totals US$8,050–$9,490 — before flights, personal expenses, visa fees, and insurance.

Every figure on this page is a real, bookable 2026 price at our English school in San Diego, five minutes from the ocean in Pacific Beach. No brochure requests, no “contact us for pricing” — the numbers are all here, and they are the numbers you pay.

Most students complete one CEFR level in about 10 weeks. Twelve weeks gives you a full level with time to consolidate it — and it is also the maximum stay on ESTA, which makes it a very typical booking at CEL San Diego.

Tuition

How Much Does an English Course in San Diego Cost Per Week?

Tuition at CEL San Diego ranges from US$300 to $410 per week, depending on course intensity and program length. The longer you book, the less you pay per week — rates drop at 7, 13, 20 and 30 weeks.

One thing is different about studying in the USA: your course determines your visa, and your visa determines your realistic course choice. Courses of 24 or more lessons per week require an F-1 visa; General English 20 can be studied on ESTA for up to 12 weeks. That is why every tier below carries a visa badge — so you budget for a course you can actually book.

More Lessons Per Week — Without an F-1 Visa

CEL’s General English 23 (GE23, 23.75 lessons per week) stays below the 24-lesson F-1 visa threshold; many students take it on ESTA for stays up to 12 weeks. Eligibility depends on nationality and U.S. entry rules.

GE23 combines the 20 General English lessons with one elective and is priced the same as General English 24. If you want to study more each week but stay on ESTA or a B1/B2 visitor visa, this is the course to budget for.

Living Costs

Is San Diego Expensive?

Plan on roughly US$2,700–$3,200 per month for tuition and accommodation on a 12-week stay, plus your personal expenses on top. San Diego is a major US coastal city, but studying here comes with two built-in savings: Pacific Beach is walkable, and the best recreation is free.

US$72/month

Transport

Most Students Walk

An MTS monthly transit pass costs US$72 — but in Pacific Beach, most students walk or bike. School, beach, cafés and nightlife sit in the same neighborhood, so many students spend close to nothing on transport.

≈US$450/month

Food & connectivity

Groceries and Your Phone

As a practical student-budget estimate, plan around US$450 per month for groceries in a shared apartment; homestay prices include meals. Basic mobile plans start around US$25 per month.

Free

Free recreation

The Beach Costs Nothing

The beach costs nothing, and neither does most of what students do here: surfing (once you rent or buy a board), Balboa Park’s grounds, and beach volleyball. If you stay in a Superior apartment, tennis, basketball and beach volleyball courts are included.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Costs

How much does it cost to study English in San Diego?

+

US$300–$410 per week in tuition, depending on course and duration. A typical 12-week stay including accommodation totals US$8,050–$9,490 — before flights, personal expenses, visa fees, and insurance.

Is it cheaper to study English for a longer period?

+

Yes. Weekly tuition drops at 7, 13, 20 and 30 weeks — General English 20 falls from US$370 per week (1–6 weeks) to US$300 per week (20–29 weeks). Accommodation works the same way: shared-apartment rates drop at 12 and 24 weeks, homestay rates at 5 and 12 weeks.

Are there additional fees besides tuition?

+

Three, all listed here: a one-time registration fee of US$150, learning materials at US$10 per week, and a one-time accommodation placement fee of US$100 (shared apartments) or US$200 (homestay). There are no amenities fees.

How much should I budget per month in San Diego?

+

Roughly US$2,700–$3,200 per month including tuition and accommodation. On top of that, shared-apartment students spend about US$450 per month on groceries; homestay includes meals.

Do I need an F-1 visa, and what does it cost?

+

Only for courses of 24 or more lessons per week (General English 24). F-1 students pay a one-time SEVIS I-901 fee of US$350 plus the US$185 visa application fee. For General English 20 or GE23 up to 12 weeks, many nationalities can travel on ESTA (US$40.27) instead. Full guide: F-1 visa for English school in San Diego.

Is health insurance included?

+

No. Health insurance is available through CEL for US$4 per day — about US$340 for a 12-week stay — or you can bring your own policy.

Is San Diego more expensive than Los Angeles or Vancouver?

+

As a rule of thumb: San Diego is cheaper than Los Angeles, and Vancouver is slightly cheaper than San Diego — though the Vancouver comparison shifts with the CAD exchange rate, and your accommodation choice, visa route and length of stay move the total more than the city does. One structural advantage in San Diego: most Pacific Beach students walk or bike, so transport can cost close to zero. Compare in detail: San Diego vs Los Angeles · cost of studying English in Vancouver.

When is the cheapest time to study in San Diego?

+

Tuition is the same all year — there is no high-season supplement on courses. Accommodation costs more during the summer high season (May 30 – September 26, 2026), so stays from autumn to spring are cheaper overall.

More on Studying English in San Diego

How can you lower the total cost of studying in San Diego?

Four levers

Duration brackets

Tuition rates drop at 7, 13, 20 and 30 weeks, shared-apartment rates at 12 and 24, homestay at 5 and 12. Booking 13 weeks instead of 12 often adds little to the total, because the weekly tuition rate falls at 13.

Season

Tuition never changes. Accommodation carries a supplement from May 30 to September 26, 2026, so an autumn-to-spring start avoids it.

Housing choice

The Standard shared apartment, or a homestay double room for two traveling together, keeps weekly costs lowest — and homestay includes meals, which removes the grocery budget.

The course itself

General English 20 covers most goals at US$300–370 per week. Budget for GE23 or GE24 only if you want more lessons each week.

And wherever you land: in Pacific Beach most students walk or bike, so transport can cost close to nothing.

How do payment and refunds work?

You receive a complete written quote before you pay anything, with every fee itemized — the same numbers published on this page. Payment schedules and refund conditions are set out in CEL’s policies.
