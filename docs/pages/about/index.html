Our mission

A welcoming, boutique school where you feel at home — and make real progress in English.

CEL opened in San Diego in 1981 with that one clear vision. Today you can study in three cities by the Pacific: San Diego, Los Angeles, and Vancouver.

A small class at the Pacific Beach campus.

After class, the beach is a five-minute walk.

Friends from five countries, one table.

Weekend excursions — hikes, tours, and trips.

Our story

More than 40 years in the making

Our passion for quality and community has let CEL grow slowly and carefully — school by school, never losing the boutique feeling.

1981

San Diego

CEL opens with one clear vision: a school where students feel at home.

2009

Los Angeles

A second school opens in Santa Monica, right by the Pacific Ocean.

2015

Pacific Beach

A second San Diego campus opens in the Pacific Beach neighborhood.

2024

Vancouver

Vancouver joins the CEL family — one of Canada's most international cities.

2025

Flagship campus

Both San Diego locations unite in a brand-new campus, minutes from the beach.

CEL remains independently owned and managed. The focus is always on what matters most: quality teaching, personal attention, and student satisfaction.

Our philosophy

Every student's journey is unique

That is why the CEL approach rests on three principles.

Small classes, big progress

An average of 6–7 students per class (maximum 12) means personal attention in every lesson.

International mix

Students from 50+ countries every year create a truly global classroom.

Immersive experience

English is more than grammar. Live the language — surf in San Diego, explore Los Angeles, hike in Vancouver.

Why CEL
